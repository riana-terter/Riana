Privacy Policy — Shoot the Ducks · Arcade Hunt

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Shoot the Ducks · Arcade Hunt is published by Riana Goot (Rianago). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress — levels unlocked, star ratings, best scores, coins, power-ups owned and your sound setting — in your device's own app storage. This data stays on your device; we cannot see it. It may be included in your device's regular backup if you have backups enabled, under your own account, governed by your device maker's terms. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads only play when you choose to watch one in the in-game shop; there are no banner or forced ads. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID and related information, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's Google settings.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games Services)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your scores can be submitted to public leaderboards, and your Play Games profile (player name and avatar) is used by Google to display them. This sign-in is handled entirely by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. If you never sign in, no scores leave your device.

What we don't collect

The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics SDK, and no server of ours. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or files, and we do not sell any data.

Children

The app is a general-audience arcade game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the ad serving described above, which is operated by Google under its own policies.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety information on Google Play.
